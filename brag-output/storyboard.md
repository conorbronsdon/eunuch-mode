# Storyboard: The Petition Desk (as built)

Direction: a candlelit ministry desk seen from above. The developer's questionable decree arrives as a petition and is stamped MOST JUDICIOUS (the ceremony); then the real run answers "No, not this sprint." (the advice), and the flattered petition is rolled back up, unread. One red wax seal is carried through every scene. Palette: ink #16120E, manila #E8D5A3, vellum #F3E6C4, wax #9E1B20, brass #B08D57, gold #E2BE74. Type: IM Fell English (the court's voice), Special Elite (petitions), JetBrains Mono (the prompt and install line). Music: "Sneaky Snitch" (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0), 87 BPM; the stamp and the hard cut land on beats.

Scene lengths are computed in `composition/index.html` from the reading rule (every line still and fully visible for at least max(1.2 s, words / 3.3 + 0.6 s), counted after it lands) and snapped to half-beats. `node render.mjs --check` prints the exact windows. Total: 34.4 s.

Compositions: the poised stamp, the stamp close-up, the stamped petition, the seal filling the frame, the tagline scroll, the prompt scroll, the flattery, the verdict, the torn page, the castle, the server, the returning petition, the rolled and sealed scroll (push-in), and the end card: 14 in 34 s. Ambient motion (candle glow, dust, a slow desk drift and film grain) plus one secondary action per hold (cursor, bowing ornament, blinking server lights, winding roller, light sheen) keep every hold alive while the text itself stays still.
